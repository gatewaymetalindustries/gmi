About Gateway

Fifty years of making tins. Still a family business.

Founded1975
Ismail Sulemanji PatrawalaFounder, Gateway Metal Industries

Gateway Metal Industries started in 1975 as a small workshop in Lower Parel, Mumbai, making tin containers for local spice merchants.

Ismail Sulemanji Patrawala set it up with one idea that still runs the place: a tin carries more than the product inside it. If a seam splits or a lid won’t hold, it’s the brand on the label that takes the hit.

Over the years the customers changed, from spice traders to paint and adhesive makers, dye and chemical companies and FMCG brands, and the range grew with them. In 2009 production moved to larger works in Taloja MIDC, Navi Mumbai, where every Gateway tin is made today.

When you call us, you speak to the people who run the factory.
