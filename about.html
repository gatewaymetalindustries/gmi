The Beginning

A Workshop.
A Vision.
A Promise.

In 1975, in a modest workshop tucked inside Mumbai's Lower Parel — then the beating industrial heart of Maharashtra — Ismail Sulemanji Patrawala set up a bench, a press, and a belief that would outlast generations. The belief that a container is never just a container. It is a promise: that what is inside will arrive exactly as it left.

"The tin doesn't carry the product. It carries the maker's reputation."

That philosophy was not a slogan. It shaped every decision — the gauge of tinplate chosen, the tightness of a seam, the precision of a lid. In an era when cutting corners was commonplace, Gateway became known as the manufacturer that simply refused to.

Word spread quietly, the way it does when quality speaks for itself. Spice traders. Chemical manufacturers. Pharmaceutical distributors. Each came looking for a tin that would not fail them. Each left with a long-term partner.

Five Decades

The Road
Here

Every milestone in our history reflects the same discipline — grow without compromising the quality that got us here in the first place.

1975
The Founding

Ismail Sulemanji Patrawala establishes Gateway Metal Industries in Lower Parel, Mumbai. The first products: bespoke tin containers for local spice and food colouring merchants who demand airtight quality on a budget that punishes failure.

1990s
Industrial Expansion

The reputation for zero-defect seams attracts India's growing industrial chemicals sector. Gateway begins producing heavy-duty tins for dyes, adhesives, and solvents — products that punish poor packaging mercilessly. Every unit holds.

2000s
FMCG & Scale

India's FMCG boom creates demand for high-volume, branded packaging. Gateway invests in new tooling and takes on its first national-scale contracts — producing millions of cookie tins, curry powder boxes, and specialty containers per year without a single compromise in tolerance.

2009
Taloja — A New Era

Anticipating the next decade of growth, Gateway relocates to a purpose-built facility in Taloja, Maharashtra. Automated production lines, expanded tooling capacity, and a modern quality-assurance lab allow us to serve global supply chains while maintaining the handcrafted precision our clients have always depended on.

Today
Fifty Years Strong

Gateway Metal Industries stands as one of India's premier tin packaging manufacturers — serving clients across food, pharma, FMCG, and industrial sectors, shipping worldwide. The workshop in Lower Parel is gone. The standard it set is not.
