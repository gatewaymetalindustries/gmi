How an order runs

From your drawing to dispatch.

Standard sizes skip straight to production. New sizes go through tooling and a sample first.

  1. 01

    Specification

    We agree the size, tinplate, lid and finish with you, from a drawing, a sample, or one of our standard tins.

  2. 02

    Tooling & sample

    For a new size we make the tooling and a sample tin. Nothing goes into production until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Production

    Sheets are cut, formed and seamed, and lids fitted, on our lines in Taloja.

  4. 04

    Checks & dispatch

    Tins are checked for seams, lid fit and finish, then packed and sent to you.
